## The feeling

A quiet black stage. One tiny change. The interface speaks to you, waits for your guess, and lets the experiment
answer. A small heart/cursor makes the exchange feel human. The scientific explanation stays calm and exact.

## Visual grammar

- Near-black stage (#090909), white text and hard white dialogue borders, generous empty space.
- A single white outlined arena or dialogue box per beat. Square corners, 2–4 px rules, no soft shadows or glows.
- Violet #8a3ffc marks the current action. Magenta #ee5396 marks the first detected change. Colour carries meaning.
- Departure Mono for short dialogue, chapter cues and the title. IBM Plex for longer explanations, tables and data.
  Keep scientific figures legible and unchanged. Pixel art should stay crisp at integer scaling.
- `*` introduces a short line of dialogue. A tiny heart or cursor marks the current action. Use it sparingly,
  especially at the first 1. It is a motif, not extra decoration scattered across every component.
- Clear IBM-style editorial alignment and disciplined spacing hold the composition together. Technical material
  appears in a precise evidence window, while the surrounding stage keeps the game-like character.

## Motion and sound

- Type short dialogue deliberately, then leave time to read. Reveal complete words in long technical captions.
- A quiet hold before Verify. Shot 1 is a muted beat. Shot 2 gets one clear magenta change, a short original chime
  and a pause long enough for the audience to understand it. The data, not effects, supplies the surprise.
- Hard cuts or short stepped transitions between scenes. Keep deliberate camera moves for artwork and heatmap
  inspection. No perpetual bounce, floating cards, particle storms or full-screen scanline filter.
- Original sparse 8-bit/triangle notes with a soft background tone. Duck under Rashida's human voice. Silence is
  part of the design. The final film retains her recorded voice and her editorial approval.

## Possible app translation after slide review

The app should feel like the same world, including before and after an action: a framed introduction, an artwork
inspection arena, square choice controls with a clear cursor/selection state, a short response after an edit, and
a distinct first-detection beat. Reuse current workflows and the five tabs. Do not add game language that changes
the scientific meaning or implies that a no-alarm result establishes equality. Preserve keyboard use and phone
layout. A silent app is fine; the video supplies the soundtrack.

## Production beats

1. Opening: the painting on a black stage. Dialogue asks whether one changed pixel can hide.
2. Title: SwapPrint, sparse framed subtitle, one small violet cursor.
3. Method: exact construction as clean editable evidence, revealed in deliberate steps.
4. Demo: framed choice, guess, Verify, first 0, first 1, hold, heatmap.
5. Rigour: neutral evidence windows with readable labels, N and intervals.
6. Close: one clear practical limitation, then the team and live app invitation.
